Pizza Margherita


Description

Pizza Margherita is the benchmark of Neapolitan pizza, designed to showcase three core ingredients mirroring the colors of the Italian flag: red tomatoes, white mozzarella, and green basil. Its brilliance relies on high heat and minimal, high-quality components rather than heavy toppings.

The crust is light, airy, and blistered, cradling a bright, lightly salted tomato sauce made from crushed San Marzano tomatoes. Fresh mozzarella melts into creamy pockets, finished with fragrant torn basil leaves and a drizzle of peppery extra virgin olive oil.


Ingredients

  • 1 prepared Neapolitan pizza dough ball (approx. 250 g)
  • 80 g canned San Marzano whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand with a pinch of salt
  • 100 g fresh mozzarella or fior di latte, drained and torn into chunks
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Semolina or flour for dusting

Steps

  1. Place a pizza stone or baking steel on the top rack of your oven and preheat to its maximum temperature (at least 250°C / 500°F, or broil setting) for at least 45–60 minutes.
  2. Dust a work surface with semolina flour. Gently press and stretch the dough from the center outward using your fingers, leaving a raised outer rim (cornicione) without using a rolling pin.
  3. Transfer the stretched base onto a lightly dusted pizza peel. Spread the crushed tomatoes evenly across the center, leaving the rim bare.
  4. Distribute the torn mozzarella evenly over the sauce and add a light drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.
  5. Slide the pizza onto the hot stone or steel. Bake until the crust is blistered and charred in spots and the cheese is melted and bubbling (about 4–7 minutes depending on oven heat).
  6. Remove from the oven, immediately scatter fresh basil leaves over the melted cheese, slice, and serve hot.
